# Lupin

## A. Introduction

**Lupin** is a hit French mystery thriller television series that premiered on Netflix in **2021**. Inspired by the iconic adventures of Arsène Lupin, the classic gentleman thief created by Maurice Leblanc, the show offers a modern, stylish take on classic heist stories. It blends suspense, clever strategy, and emotional depth into an engaging mystery drama.

---

## B. Synopsis

The story follows Assane Diop, a man whose life was upended as a teenager when his father was framed for the theft of an expensive diamond necklace by his wealthy and powerful employer, Hubert Pellegrini. After his father dies tragically in prison, Assane grows up fueled by a desire for justice and revenge. Drawing inspiration from a book about Arsène Lupin that his father gave him, Assane adopts the persona and techniques of the legendary "gentleman thief." Using cunning disguises, master sleight-of-hand, and brilliant strategic planning, he sets out to expose Pellegrini's crimes and clear his father's name. As Assane executes high-stakes heists across Paris, he must constantly stay one step ahead of both the police and Pellegrini's dangerous henchmen while protecting his own family.

---

## C. Characters

* **Assane Diop:** A brilliant, charismatic gentleman thief determined to avenge his father and expose corporate corruption.
* **Claire:** Assane’s devoted ex-girlfriend and the mother of his son, Raoul, who remains his anchor throughout his turbulent life.
* **Youssef Guedira:** An observant police officer and fellow Lupin enthusiast who is the first to connect Assane's heists to the classic novels.
* **Hubert Pellegrini:** A ruthless, wealthy tycoon who framed Assane's father to cover up his own financial fraud.
* **Babakar Diop:** Assane’s late father, whose tragic framing for theft serves as the primary motivation for Assane’s crusade.

---

## D. Personal Review

### Why I Chose This Series
I chose *Lupin* because of its clever writing, slick production style, and refreshing modern spin on a classic literary character.

### What I Liked About It
1. **Intricate Heist Schemes:** The show excels at showing Assane outsmarting his enemies using intellect and misdirection rather than brute force.
2. **Charismatic Lead Performance:** Omar Sy brings incredible charm, warmth, and screen presence to the role of Assane Diop.
3. **Pacing and Cliffhangers:** Each episode is tightly plotted with twists that keep you hooked from start to finish.

### Rating
> **Personal Rating:** 9 / 10

### Recommendation
If you enjoy clever heist thrillers, mystery dramas, and charismatic main characters, you should definitely watch *Lupin*.
